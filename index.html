Selection, not administrative boundaries

This version works with postal localities. Places are grouped by country, administrative fields and exact locality name. Their reference point is a spherical mean of distinct postal coordinates. It is neither an official town center nor a municipal centroid.

“Places → all their known PLZ” selects those reference points and then returns the selected localities’ full known postal records. Some returned postal coordinates can therefore lie outside the circle. “Postal points” filters each postal coordinate directly.

Privacy

    
Radius Atlas has no account, cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. Queries and exports run in your browser and are not sent to a geocoder or AI service. The GitHub Pages host may retain ordinary request metadata such as IP address and timestamps.

    
When the street basemap is enabled, your browser requests visible map tiles directly from the configured provider. That provider receives normal connection data and the requested tile area. Turn off “Street basemap” to stop new tile requests.
